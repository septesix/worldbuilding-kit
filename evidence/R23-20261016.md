# R-23 — `beat_budget` 45 → 60, measured rather than felt

Date: 2026-10-16 · guidance key: **`cf1f1a6e`** (was `5e29a642`) · suite 199/0

## How the numbers were obtained

Without recounting beats myself — reimplementing a grader's arithmetic is how two earlier findings turned
out to be about the instrument. `invariants.py` prints a beat **only when it exceeds the cap**, so the cap
was forced to 1 in throwaway copies under `/tmp` (rewriting `beat_budget:` in the dumped world index, or
appending `beat_budget=1` to the label where a dump carries none), and every beat in the corpus then names
itself. 627 beats across the skill-arm multi-turn records:

| beat | n | median | p90 | p95 | max | share over 45 | share over 60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `question` | 112 | 14 | 21 | 24 | 29 | 0% | 0% |
| `digest` | 109 | 22 | 34 | 36 | 42 | 0% | 0% |
| `stop` | 102 | 9 | 21 | 23 | 40 | 0% | 0% |
| `target` | 107 | 27 | 52 | 56 | 78 | 20% | 3% |
| `write back` | 101 | 40 | 56 | 70 | 106 | 30% | 7% |
| `reflect` | 96 | 41 | 64 | 72 | 107 | **37%** | 12% |

The reading that changed the decision: **45 was biting the beats that are supposed to carry content.**
`reflect` ("state one consequence the writer did not say") and `write back` ("what was filed where") are the
two beats the turn contract *requires* to say something, and their medians sit at 40–41 — one word from the
cap. Meanwhile the three lean beats never approached it, so the cap was not protecting the writer from
monologues; it was shaving the part of the turn that does the work. The monologues live at p99 (104, 107),
about 1–2% of beats, and any cap in this range still fails them.

Also: R-10's stated diagnosis — "the fat sits in `target` and `write back`" — was half wrong. `target` runs
at median 27. The offender was `reflect`, which R-10 didn't list.

## Decision and cost

60 keeps every lean beat untouched (they exceeded 45 in 0% of beats), cuts the false flags on `reflect` from
37% to 12% and `write back` from 30% to 7%, and still fails a 90-word beat. Cost if wrong: four 55-word
beats in one turn pass the beat rule — accepted, because `words_per_turn` (170) is the bound on exactly
that sum and is enforced per turn; the beat cap's job is the single-gulp shape.

Chosen over the alternative (per-beat budgets for all six beats) because it is one number the writer can
edit in `world/index.md`, and nothing in the corpus shows the lean beats needing protection from a shared
cap.
