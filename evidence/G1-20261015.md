# G1 — one shape for the `provoked:` field

Date: 2026-10-15 · guidance key: **`5fc04d12`** (was `e571ec50`) · spec_rev: R-15, unchanged

## What was wrong

The field that records what provoked a turn was documented in three shapes at once, in files the model
reads in the same session:

| where | shape it showed |
|---|---|
| `SKILL.md:59` | `provoked: <type>` - one type, singular |
| `assets/index-template.md:64` | an eight-name enum that listed `review` as a *type*, alongside `constraint` and `stake` |
| `references/voice.md:28` | "≥1 of five types" - five, not eight |
| `SKILL.md:26`, `references/rubric.md:109,110,136` | the literal `provoked: review` |

So the vocabulary was either five or eight depending on the file, and `review` was either a type or a
whole entry. Real runs had noticed and picked two different answers themselves: across the corpus the
field appears as `provoked: review` 12 times and as `provoked: discovery, review` 3 times. `constraint`
and `stake` appear in 0 real logs - they exist only in that template enum.

This is the ground that the false review finding of 2026-10-13 grew on. The detector has since been made
to accept every spelling, so nothing here changes any verdict already recorded; what changes is that the
guidance now states one thing, and the treadmill check keeps working when a review happens, because a memo
turn now records its real type alongside the flag.

## What changed

- The canonical line: `- turn: <n> · provoked: <types> · targeted: <pillar>`, where `<types>` is one or
  more of the five engagement types, plus `review` on a memo turn. `review` is a flag, not a sixth type.
- Stated in all four places a model might read it - `SKILL.md` (bookkeeping, and the `continue` trigger),
  `references/rubric.md` (the "when" and the "log it" rules), `references/voice.md` (the engagement
  contract), `assets/index-template.md` (the template's log-shape section) - because a rule in one file is
  a suggestion, and in four it is the shape of the ledger.
- The review trigger now reads "at ≥3 sessions since the last machine line names `review`", and the
  reset rule "names `review` in its `provoked:` list".
- Two I5 phrase guards re-aimed to the new wording, same intent: the trigger is named, it sits in the
  `continue` procedure, and it states the number it checks.
- New guard: no shipped file may show the old singular shape or claim "one of the eight types", and at
  least four must carry the phrase `one or more of the five`. Proved able to fail first - reverting
  `voice.md` alone produced `FAIL ... described in 3 files with 1 stale shape(s) left`, restoring it went
  green. That guard had to be installed twice: the first attempt died on a quoting error in its anchor and
  I reported the suite green without it, which is how it would have stayed unpinned.
- `SKILL.md` went to 517 words under the edit and came back to 499 by trimming, which cost one guard:
  shortening "never delete **one** under `world/`" tripped the F6 file-level check. The guard was right -
  the word "one" is what carries the rule - and the trim was reverted rather than the guard.

Suite: 184 to **185 assertions**, 0 failed.
