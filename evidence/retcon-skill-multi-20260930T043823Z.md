## Canon
- proposed: nobody eats what the tollhouse inspects; fish is cooked below the lock, never at it.
- proposed: every house keeps one lamp unlit in a window, called the fourth watch, and nobody claims who set the custom.

- proposed: names are not used at work; on the water you are hand, voice, or the one who came in last.
