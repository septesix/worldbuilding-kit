# G4 — the close recap gets a shape, so its exemption is real

Date: 2026-10-15 · guidance key: **`5e29a642`** (was `c8359411`)

## What was wrong

Guidance promised the session-close recap is an appendix - excluded from `words_per_turn` - and named it in
four files without ever saying what a recap bullet looks like. The instrument that grants the exemption
keys on bullets that open with their source (`` - `file`, `T-014` — ``), and runs wrote citations at the
*end* of long sentences. So the promise was unenforceable and the exemption invisible: close turns were
graded as prose. In the certification batch, `close/skill` breached at 175 and 256 words on turns that were
carrying exempt recap all along.

Measured directly with the counter, on identical content:

| six bullets, ~90 words of recap | turn words counted | bullets exempted |
|---|---|---|
| citation at the end (what runs wrote) | **135** | 0 |
| citation first (what guidance now says) | **9** | 6 |

126 words of the difference was the same text on either side of a comma.

## What changed

"citation first" - each bullet opens with the `file` or `T-num` it records, then one clause about what
changed - stated in `SKILL.md`, in `voice.md`'s appendix clause, and in `rubric.md`'s closing section with
the reason: a bullet whose source sits at the end cannot be told apart from turn prose by the reader, or by
the counter that grants the exemption. Memo items were left alone: the shape cap is already stated where it
belongs (`rubric.md:142`), and one run exceeding it is a compliance question, not a missing rule.

Guards: the phrase in ≥3 shipped files, plus two-sided instrument tests - the citation-first form must
produce 6 exempted bullets and a low prose count, and the trailing form must produce 0 exempted and a high
count. Without the second half the first half proves nothing, because a counter that exempts everything
would also "work". `SKILL.md` 499 words.

## Verification

`close skill 12` (a real session that reaches `turns_per_session` and closes), 12 turns, 36 minutes:

- **The recap took the documented shape.** All six bullets led with `` `file` `` + `` `T-num` ``. The close
  turn counted 125 words of prose with 6 bullets exempted, inside the cap ✓. Graded per-turn, wordcount
  reports the 6; the aggregate line shows the worst turn's aux counts, which is why a full-file run reads
  "0 recap bullets" - the per-turn figure is the one to quote.
- **G6 held in the same run**: one machine line per turn that happened, no back-filled history. One turn
  in twelve still went unlogged (`targeted: logged in 11/12 turns`).
- **Two results that are not good**: mean 184 words a turn, **10 of 12 over the cap**, and a consecutive
  pillar repeat ending at turns 2 and 7. The length comparison rule works in five-turn sessions (retcon:
  5/5 over before, 2/5 after) and stops working in twelve-turn ones; the cadence rule survives short
  sessions and not long ones. Both are recorded rather than fixed here.

The run also produced something that had nothing to do with G4 and had to be retracted before I could
report it - see `CANON-20261015.md`.
