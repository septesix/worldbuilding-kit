# R-27 — the "you pick" branch, and the instrument bug it surfaced

2026-10-16. Guidance batch: key `23e6e3af → f403e816` (SKILL.md 499 words). Suite 209 → **217**, CI green.
Spec open item 1 is closed. The headline finding is not the branch: it is that **every multi-turn
turn-word number in this repo since R-24 was inflated by the harness's own prompt text.**

## What the branch is

§2.2 promised "or *you pick* → deal three premises off different genre assumptions". No guidance file
contained it, so in practice a writer who declined got the same question back — the writer ends up
authoring a blank page twice, which is the failure mode the branch existed to prevent.

The shape, so it is checkable instead of aspirational: three premises, one per axis rather than one per
adjective —

- `power` — who decides, and what they cannot decide
- `cost` — what the world charges for being itself
- `belief` — what everyone assumes that could turn out to be wrong

two sentences each, then one question: which, or none. **Prose, not an appendix** — they count against
`words_per_turn`, which is the reason a premise is two sentences and not a paragraph. I did not exempt
them the way R-25 exempted the sketch: an exemption wants a measured distribution behind it, and this one
has zero samples. Stated in `SKILL.md` (mode bullet), `references/rubric.md` (mechanics plus the
fake-choice warning: change the axis, not the adjective), and `tests/prompts/you-pick.txt`, which is wired
into `run.sh` so certification exercises it. A guard counts the shipped places and fails below three.

`invariants` flags bit 4 when a turn's writer line declines, **nothing** was offered, and the assistant's
text asks for a premise. Silence when premises or the ordinary `steady/sharp/strange` trio appear, so
"up to you" about *which option* is not mistaken for declining the *premise*. Proven on three fixtures:
asked-back → set; three dealt → clear; option-trio deflection → clear.

## The bug that fell out

To write that flag I had to read the writer's line, and `is_writer_echo` returned nothing for it:
`turns_of` splits on the turn header **including its digits**, so the block begins with a bare `1` and the
`^## turn` pattern the helper looks for above the echo never exists. From R-24's positional rewrite until
this commit, every `writer:` prompt echo in a multi-turn artifact was counted as assistant prose.

| reading | multi/skill n | median worst-of-nothing | % over cap | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre-R-24 (shape-exempt) | 119 | ~158 | 41% | reaction-mode bullets invisible |
| R-24 → R-27 (positional, header-blind) | 121 | 172 | **58%** | prompt echoes charged to the model |
| R-27 (positional, archive-aware) | 119 | **161** | **43%** | echo excluded by position, header absence tolerated |

Single-turn runs (median 127, 23%) were never affected: they parse through the branch where the header
survives. That asymmetry is why the R-24→R-27 number looked *more* damning than the truth — and why the
58% I quoted in `R25-20261016.md` and in the commit message was wrong within hours of being written.

**13 recorded verdicts moved, all downward** (11 × `invariants` — ten by −4, one by −5 — and 2 ×
`wordcount`, 1 → 0). Polarity,
because that is the question that matters: the moves are a BAD turn no longer passing as over-cap on words
the model never wrote. Nothing went flagged→clean by acquiring an exemption; the two `wordcount` files'
worst turns are 156w and 158w against a 170 cap once the echo is out, and the largest prompt echoes in those
runs are 12 and 21 words — echo-sized, so the move is explained by the defect rather than by a new exemption. `golden_verdicts.tsv` re-recorded, 216 rows.

Two more instances of the same family, found while proving the first: `recap` and `sketch_lines` receive
`body` — the block with echoes already stripped — so a close announced *by the writer's own wrap-up line*
was invisible to them. Consequence: `check.sh`'s k4 fixture (5 turns, writer says "that's all I have for
today, wrap it up", model recaps) read as a sketch overrun, i.e. a **good run failing**. `turn_is_close()`
now asks once, against the raw block, and the helpers take the answer.

**Rejected on the way:** a blanket "if this is the close turn, slug bullets are the recap" exemption. It
fixes k4 and opens a hole — 6 close-turn bullets at any length would escape `words_per_turn` and
`sketch_words` alike, which is bad-runs-pass. The position-only fix leaves a real sketch in a close turn as
prose, still counted.

## Method note, kept because I made the same mistake twice in one day

The guard for the echo rule (`writer:` lines are excluded by position) was green for the entire period the
rule was broken, because its fixture is a `## transcript` file and that path keeps the header line. A
synthetic fixture tests the branch you wrote; the corpus lane tests the branch the harness produces. There
are now two archive-shaped echo guards next to the transcript one — the exclusion holds at 0 words' delta
across a 15-word change in the echo, and a mid-turn `writer:` line still adds its 23 words.
