# R-25 / R-26 — reaction mode gets a bound, options get the ceiling they already have

2026-10-16. Guidance batch: key `cf1f1a6e -> 23e6e3af` (SKILL.md 498 words). Suite 207 → **209**, green.
Two operator decisions, both taken from measurement rather than from taste.

## R-25: `sketch_words: 25`, six lines to a block

**The decision.** Reaction mode's sketch gets its own tunable instead of borrowing `words_per_turn`.
This is the resolution of what R-24 uncovered: the fixed counter made the beat that `SKILL.md` *asks
for* ("sketch 6 lines") count as ordinary conversational prose, and the one sketch on record does not
fit — turn 5 of `cadence-skill-multi-20261015T035948Z.md` reads 291w against a 170 cap when its six
sketch lines are treated as prose.

**What was measured.** Every multi/skill artifact, assistant-prose region only (the first pass also
swept in `threads.md` dumps from the artifact tail and reported 30 blocks in 12 runs; that number was
wrong by an order of magnitude and the instrument's own sectioning fixed it):

- **one** sketch block exists in the corpus: 6 lines, 17–20 words each, median 18.
- so `sketch_words` is set as headroom over a single observation (25 over a max of 20), labelled
  provisional in `docs/tunables.md`, with the re-measure trigger stated (5 more reaction-mode turns).

**What shipped.** A sketch block is four or more consecutive bullets that each open with a bare `slug`,
in a turn that is not a close (`harness_markers.sketch_lines()`, one implementation shared by both
gates). Those lines go to a new bucket: exempt from `words_per_turn`, bounded by `sketch_words` per line
and by six in count, breach reported under bit 2 (appendix) like the other appendices. Stated in three
shipped places: `SKILL.md` (the beat line), `references/rubric.md` (a section: why the exemption and why
six), `assets/index-template.md` (the tunable with its measured basis). Fixture carries both keys.

**Cost, honestly stated.** A turn can now carry 6 × 25 = 150 exempted words on top of its conversational
budget. That is bounded by the count rule and the per-line rule, not by a sum, and it is the same trade
already accepted for `beat_budget` in R-23. What is *not* true is that the change loosened the length
rule: across the corpus, turn-word statistics barely moved (multi/skill median 173 → 172, still 58% over
its own cap; single/skill 131 → 127, 26% → 23%).

**The one verdict that moved.** `golden_verdicts.tsv` re-recorded: **1 of 213**.
`pressure-wrapup-skill-20260930T023105Z.md` / wordcount: `rc=1 → rc=2`. Measured with the instrument
rather than a reimplementation (monkeypatching `sketch_lines` to return nothing and re-running
`wordcount.measure`), the reason is exact: that file's worst turn was 181w with the four sketch lines as
prose and is 91w with them as an appendix, and two of those lines are 31w and 26w — over `sketch_words`.
So the overrun is still reported, now attributed to the bound it actually violates. No file went from
flagged to clean: cadence turn 5 remains over `words_per_turn` (171w) even with its sketch exempt.

## R-26: `option_words: 18 → 19`, and the reading is per option

**The decision.** "19 words is fine" — so the cap moves to the ceiling the behaviour has, and the rule
that governs is the per-option one, not the longest-option-per-turn column.

**What was measured.** All 318 options in the multi/skill corpus: min 7, median 13, p90 17, p95 17,
**p99 19, max 19**. At cap 18: 4 of 318 options over (1%), 2 of 17 runs contain one. At 19: 0 of 318,
0 of 17 runs. So the two columns stop disagreeing — the flag and the column both report 0% over, and 0%
is now a statement about behaviour rather than about which column was read.

Cost if wrong: a 20-word option passes unnoticed, and 19 is exactly the observed max, so the first new
run that reaches for a 20-word option is the one that tests this. `words_per_turn` still bounds the turn
the options sit in.

## Two instrument fixes that fell out of reading my own code

- `wordcount` compared options against a **literal 18**, not `option_words`. A world that changed the
  tunable moved one gate and not the other. Both caps now come from the artifact (`option_cap`,
  `sketch_cap`), and `invariants` calls `wc.option_cap` instead of keeping its own copy.
- the F1 both-sided guard's premise was superseded by this batch: its negative fixture (six slug
  bullets, no close) is now correctly a sketch. Replaced by three-sided guards — clean sketch exempt and
  unflagged; a 26-word line fails `sketch_words` *and* stays out of the turn count; a seven-line block
  fails the count. Plus the two that were already there: documented recap in a close → exempt; the same
  words citation-last → counted.

## Found while doing this, deliberately not fixed here

**F7 — `invariants` exits 0 on a file it could not grade.** On `pressure-wrapup-…` it prints
`skip  no turns recorded (single-turn file or aborted run)` and returns 0, so "graded nothing" and
"graded clean" share the byte. `wordcount` already reserves 64 for exactly this (`NOT_GRADED`) and the
project's standing rule is that a missing witness must fail, not skip. Every single-turn artifact in
`golden_verdicts.tsv` is therefore recorded as clean-by-invariants, which is the folklore shape this
whole batch is meant to eliminate. Fixing it means (a) returning 64 when no turn block parsed and (b)
teaching the aggregates and the provenance tool to treat 64 as *not measured* rather than as a breach —
which is a behaviour change in the corpus lane and wants its own commit, not a ride-along.
